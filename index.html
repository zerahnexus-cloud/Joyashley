One chapter ends. A bigger journey begins.

My Grade 9 journey has been more than completing junior school. It has been a season of discovering my voice, building confidence, exploring my talents and understanding the possibilities that lie ahead.

02 GRATITUDE

Thank you for giving us room to grow.

My journey through junior school has been shaped by people, opportunities and experiences that helped me discover more about myself.

I am grateful to Peter Kibukosya Comprehensive School for creating opportunities to learn, explore and participate beyond the classroom. I am equally grateful for the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), which has encouraged us to learn through practical projects, creativity, collaboration and real-life experiences.

These experiences have shown me that education is not only about what we know. It is also about what we can create, how we connect with others and how confidently we use what we have learned.

Confidence is a journey.
DISCOVERY

Confidence can change everything.

Along the way, I discovered something that changed how I see school, talent and personal growth: confidence matters.

When a student believes in their ability to speak, create, participate and try something new, learning becomes a different experience.

Confidence can influence our wellbeing, our willingness to participate and even how we perform. It gives us the courage to ask questions, share ideas, discover our strengths and learn from mistakes.

I have learned that confidence is not about being the loudest person in the room. Sometimes, it simply means having the courage to believe that your voice, your ideas and your talents matter.

THE BLUEPRINT

I don't just want to move on. I want to leave something behind.

As I prepare to transition to higher education, I have been thinking about the students who will come after me. I want my journey to become a starting point for someone else's.

My vision is simple: create opportunities where students can discover their interests, develop their talents, express themselves and gain the confidence to dream bigger.

ANOTHER DISCOVERY

It started with a camera.

Sometimes a talent begins with something as simple as being given the chance to try.

I had the opportunity to take photographs using a digital camera, and that experience opened my eyes to the world of media and journalism.

I began to see photography differently — not just as taking pictures, but as a way of capturing moments, telling stories and helping people see the world through another person's eyes.

It reminded me that young people need opportunities to experiment. A simple opportunity can reveal a talent that might otherwise remain undiscovered.
